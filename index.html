Compliance boundary

AFSS Ready NSW is a coordination tool only. It does not provide fire-safety certification, legal advice, engineering advice, accredited practitioner assessment or official council/FRNSW lodgement.

AFSS Ready NSW is an administrative coordination tool. Building owners should rely on qualified professionals, accredited practitioners and relevant authorities for compliance decisions.
